Work with me

I take on a small number of advisory engagements alongside my role at the International Vaccine Institute. I help teams design, run and interpret vaccine studies in low- and middle-income countries, from the first concept note to the policy brief.

Book an introductory call Download CV

How I can help

Vaccine clinical development strategy

Clinical development plans, trial sequencing from Phase I to III, and regulatory documents such as clinical study reports and Common Technical Document sections.

Trial and study design

Protocols, statistical analysis plans and sample size for individually and cluster randomized trials, household transmission studies and immunogenicity studies.

Vaccine effectiveness and impact evaluation

Test-negative, cohort and case-control designs, real-world evidence, and serology-based measures of programme performance.

Surveillance and site readiness in Africa and Asia

Multi-country disease surveillance, site capacity assessment and partner coordination for outbreak-ready research.

Scientific review and expert panels

Data safety monitoring boards, protocol and grant review, and technical advisory groups.

Training and workshops

Short courses for research teams on trial design, sample size, vaccine epidemiology and scientific writing.

Track record

  • Led clinical development work on a typhoid conjugate vaccine that is now licensed.
  • Scientific and technical lead for a Phase Ib schistosomiasis vaccine trial in Burkina Faso and Madagascar.
  • Principal investigator on grants from CEPI, the Gates Foundation and EDCTP, including a €3.9 million trial.
  • Studies in 9 countries across Africa and Asia, and 119 peer-reviewed publications.

How an engagement works

  1. Introductory call

    A free 20-minute call to understand your question, timeline and constraints.

  2. Scope and proposal

    A short written proposal with deliverables, timeline and fees.

  3. Delivery

    Work in agreed stages, with a final report or documents your team can use directly.
