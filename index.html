Mortality Context

Life expectancy in Africa is still low, averaging about 64 years across the continent.

That is more than a decade lower than other world regions, where life expectancy has largely converged around 75-80 years.

While progress has been made over recent decades, this persistent gap reflects overlapping burdens from infectious diseases, maternal and child mortality, and uneven access to essential health services.
