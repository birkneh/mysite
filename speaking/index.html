Short bio

Birkneh T. Tadesse, MD, PhD, is Associate Director General at the International Vaccine Institute in Seoul, Visiting Professor at the Heidelberg Institute of Global Health and Affiliated Researcher at Karolinska Institutet. A pediatrician and epidemiologist, he designs and leads multi-country vaccine trials and surveillance studies across Africa and Asia, with work spanning typhoid, schistosomiasis, mpox, COVID-19 and HIV. He has led grants from CEPI, the Gates Foundation and EDCTP and has published more than 110 peer-reviewed papers.
